## Communications Objective

Provide clear, calm, factual communication to affected customers, employees, regulators, and customer-support teams. Avoid speculation, blame, or technical detail that creates security risk. Align all messages with DPO, Legal, Compliance, and CISO decisions.

## Confirmed Facts Safe to Use

- Trade Republic identified unauthorised copying of customer payment-card reconciliation data to an external storage device.
- The incident was detected by internal security monitoring.
- The employee account involved has been disabled.
- The relevant workstation has been isolated.
- The investigation is ongoing.
- The affected data may include identity, contact, and payment-card data.
- CVV, PIN, passwords, and MFA secrets are not currently indicated as affected.
- Customers should be alert to phishing and should never share PINs, passwords, TANs, or security codes.

## Facts Not Yet Safe to Use

- Do not state that fraud has occurred.
- Do not state that data was publicly leaked.
- Do not state that the employee acted with criminal intent until Legal confirms wording.
- Do not promise that no further data is affected.
- Do not promise card replacement until processor and CISO approve.
- Do not identify the employee.

## Internal Holding Message

Subject: Confidential incident response coordination - customer data security incident

We are currently investigating a customer data security incident involving unauthorised copying of payment-card reconciliation data to external storage. Security monitoring detected the event, the involved account has been disabled, and the relevant workstation has been isolated.

Please do not discuss this incident outside the response team. Any external, customer, media, or regulator communication must be coordinated through ISO, Legal, DPO, Compliance, and Communications.

Customer-facing teams should not speculate. Approved support guidance will follow.

## Customer Notification Draft

Subject: Important information about your Trade Republic card data

Dear customer,

We are contacting you because we identified a security incident that may involve some of your personal data connected to your Trade Republic card.

What happened:

Our security monitoring detected that a file containing customer payment-card reconciliation data was copied to an unauthorised external storage device. We immediately disabled the involved account, isolated the affected workstation, and started an investigation.

What data may be affected:

The data may include your name, contact details, customer identifier, card number, card expiry date, and related card-account information. Based on our current investigation, your PIN, CVV, password, MFA secrets, and app login credentials are not affected.

What we are doing:

We are investigating the incident, working with relevant internal and external specialists, and coordinating appropriate protective measures. We are also informing the competent authorities where required.

What you can do:

- Be alert to phishing emails, SMS, or calls that refer to your card or Trade Republic account.
- Do not share passwords, PINs, TANs, CVV, or security codes with anyone.
- Review card transactions and report anything suspicious immediately.
- Use only the official Trade Republic app or website to contact support.

We regret this incident and will provide updates if the investigation identifies additional actions you should take.

Contact:

For questions, contact customer support through the official app. You may also contact our Data Protection Officer at the address provided in our privacy notice.

## Customer Support Talking Points

- Acknowledge concern.
- Confirm the customer should use only official channels.
- Do not confirm individual inclusion unless the approved customer list is loaded into the support tool.
- Escalate fraud reports immediately.
- Do not ask customers for passwords, PINs, TANs, CVV, or security codes.

## Media Holding Statement

Trade Republic is investigating a security incident involving unauthorised copying of payment-card reconciliation data. We detected the incident through internal security monitoring and took immediate steps to contain it. The investigation is ongoing, and we are coordinating with the relevant authorities where required. We will inform affected customers directly.

## Communications Risks

- Customers may confuse PAN exposure with compromise of app login credentials.
- Overly technical explanations may reduce clarity.
- Premature attribution to the employee may create legal exposure.
- Delayed customer communication may increase reputational harm if the incident becomes public first.

## Communications Recommendation

Prepare customer notification now and hold for CISO, Legal, DPO, and Compliance approval. If the notification decision is positive, communicate directly to affected customers as soon as the customer list and mitigation measures are ready.
